# Cognitive Modeling Knowledge Base Glossary

This comprehensive glossary provides definitions and cross-references for key terms used throughout the cognitive modeling knowledge base. Terms are organized alphabetically and include domain-specific contexts where relevant.

## A

### Active Inference
**Definition**: A theoretical framework proposing that all cognitive processes can be understood as inference on generative models of the world, where action is inference about preferred outcomes.

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Mathematics, Systems Theory
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/free_energy_principle|Free Energy Principle]], [[cognitive/generative_model|Generative Model]], [[systems/emergence|Emergence]]

### Adaptive Systems
**Definition**: Systems that can modify their behavior or structure in response to environmental changes to improve performance.

**Domains**: Systems Theory, Biology, Engineering
**Related Terms**: [[systems/self_organization|Self-Organization]], Evolutionary Adaptation, Feedback Loops

### Agent-Based Modeling
**Definition**: Computational method for simulating complex systems by representing individual agents and their interactions.

**Domains**: Systems Theory, Computer Science, Biology
**Related Terms**: Multi-Agent Systems, [[knowledge_base/cognitive/swarm_intelligence|Swarm Intelligence]], [[mathematics/complex_systems|Complex Systems]]

### Allostasis
**Definition**: The process by which the body achieves stability through change, anticipating and adapting to future demands.

**Domains**: Biology, Physiology, Psychology
**Related Terms**: [[biology/homeostasis|Homeostasis]], Stress Response, Adaptive Capacity

## B

### Bayesian Inference
**Definition**: Statistical method for updating beliefs about unknown quantities based on observed data using Bayes' theorem.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Statistics, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/bayes_theorem|Bayes' Theorem]], [[cognitive/probabilistic_inference|Probabilistic Inference]], Posterior Distribution

### Belief Propagation
**Definition**: Algorithm for computing marginal probabilities in graphical models through iterative message passing.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Computer Science, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/graphical_models|Graphical Models]], [[cognitive/message_passing|Message Passing]], [[mathematics/factor_graphs|Factor Graphs]]

### Biological Network
**Definition**: Complex interconnected system of biological entities (genes, proteins, cells) that interact to perform functions.

**Domains**: Biology, Systems Biology, Mathematics
**Related Terms**: [[biology/gene_regulatory_networks|Gene Regulatory Networks]], [[biology/metabolic_networks|Metabolic Networks]], [[knowledge_base/systems/network_theory|Network Theory]]

### Bounded Rationality
**Definition**: Theory that decision-making is limited by cognitive constraints, information availability, and time.

**Domains**: Psychology, Economics, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/decision_making|Decision Making]], Cognitive Biases, [[mathematics/optimization_theory|Optimization Theory]]

## C

### Cellular Automaton
**Definition**: Discrete model of computation consisting of a grid of cells that evolve according to simple rules.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics
**Related Terms**: [[systems/complex_systems|Complex Systems]], [[mathematics/emergence|Emergence]], [[systems/self_organization|Self-Organization]]

### Cognitive Architecture
**Definition**: Theoretical framework specifying the structure and functioning of the mind, including memory, perception, and reasoning systems.

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Psychology, Computer Science
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/cognitive_architecture|Cognitive Architecture]], [[cognitive/working_memory|Working Memory]], Executive Function

### Collective Intelligence
**Definition**: Intelligence that emerges from the collaboration and competition of many individuals.

**Domains**: Systems Theory, Biology, Social Science
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/swarm_intelligence|Swarm Intelligence]], [[systems/emergence|Emergence]], Social Behavior

### Complex Adaptive System
**Definition**: System composed of many interacting agents that adapt and evolve in response to environmental changes.

**Domains**: Systems Theory, Biology, Social Science
**Related Terms**: [[systems/complex_systems|Complex Systems]], [[systems/adaptive_systems|Adaptive Systems]], [[biology/evolutionary_dynamics|Evolutionary Dynamics]]

### Conditional Independence
**Definition**: Property where two variables are independent given knowledge of a third variable.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Statistics, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/graphical_models|Graphical Models]], Bayesian Theory, Causal Inference

### Consciousness
**Definition**: State of being aware of and responsive to one's surroundings and internal states.

**Domains**: Philosophy, Cognitive Science, Neuroscience
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/consciousness|Consciousness]], [[philosophy/philosophy_of_mind|Philosophy of Mind]], [[cognitive/self_awareness|Self-Awareness]]

### Control Theory
**Definition**: Mathematical study of how to manipulate inputs to achieve desired outputs in dynamic systems.

**Domains**: Engineering, Mathematics, Systems Theory
**Related Terms**: Feedback Control, [[mathematics/dynamical_systems|Dynamical Systems]], Cybernetics

### Convergence
**Definition**: Process by which different methods or systems arrive at the same result or understanding.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Cognitive Science, Systems Theory
**Related Terms**: Limit Theory, Learning Convergence, System Stability

## D

### Decision Theory
**Definition**: Mathematical study of how agents make choices under uncertainty to achieve goals.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Economics, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/decision_making|Decision Making]], Utility Theory, [[mathematics/game_theory|Game Theory]]

### Dynamical Systems
**Definition**: Mathematical framework for studying systems that change over time, often described by differential equations.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Physics, Biology
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/differential_equations|Differential Equations]], System Dynamics, Physiological Dynamics

### Dynamic Causal Modeling
**Definition**: Framework for inferring hidden neuronal states and their connectivity from observed brain activity.

**Domains**: Neuroscience, Cognitive Science, Mathematics
**Related Terms**: Neural Modeling, Causal Inference, [[systems/network_dynamics|Network Dynamics]]

## E

### Ecological Network
**Definition**: Representation of feeding relationships and energy flows between species in an ecosystem.

**Domains**: Biology, Ecology, Systems Theory
**Related Terms**: Food Webs, [[systems/network_theory|Network Theory]], Ecosystem Dynamics

### Emergence
**Definition**: Phenomenon where complex patterns and behaviors arise from simple rules and interactions.

**Domains**: Systems Theory, Physics, Biology
**Related Terms**: [[systems/emergence|Emergence]], [[mathematics/complex_systems|Complex Systems]], [[cognitive/collective_behavior|Collective Behavior]]

### Entropy
**Definition**: Measure of disorder or uncertainty in a system, with applications in thermodynamics and information theory.

**Domains**: Physics, Mathematics, Information Theory
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/information_theory|Information Theory]], [[mathematics/thermodynamics|Thermodynamics]], System Disorder

### Epistemology
**Definition**: Branch of philosophy concerned with the nature, origin, and limits of knowledge.

**Domains**: Philosophy, Cognitive Science, Mathematics
**Related Terms**: Theory of Knowledge, Knowledge Representation, Proof Theory

### Evolutionary Algorithm
**Definition**: Optimization algorithm inspired by biological evolution, using selection, mutation, and recombination.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Biology, Mathematics
**Related Terms**: [[biology/evolutionary_dynamics|Evolutionary Dynamics]], [[mathematics/optimization_theory|Optimization Theory]], Genetic Algorithms

### Expected Free Energy
**Definition**: Information-theoretic quantity that guides action selection by minimizing surprise about future outcomes.

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Mathematics, Systems Theory
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/free_energy_principle|Free Energy Principle]], [[cognitive/action_selection|Action Selection]], [[knowledge_base/research/concepts/policy_optimization|Policy Optimization]]

### Expert System
**Definition**: AI system that emulates human expert decision-making using knowledge bases and inference rules.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Cognitive Science, Engineering
**Related Terms**: Knowledge-Based Systems, Rule-Based Systems, Reasoning

## F

### Factor Graph
**Definition**: Graphical representation of factorization in probability distributions, used for efficient inference.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Computer Science, Statistics
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/graphical_models|Graphical Models]], [[cognitive/message_passing|Message Passing]], [[mathematics/probabilistic_graphical_models|Probabilistic Graphical Models]]

### Feedback Loop
**Definition**: Circular causal connection where a system's output affects its input, either amplifying (positive) or dampening (negative) change.

**Domains**: Systems Theory, Biology, Engineering
**Related Terms**: Feedback Control, [[biology/homeostasis|Homeostasis]], System Stability

### Free Energy Principle
**Definition**: Principle that adaptive systems minimize variational free energy to maintain their organization.

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Mathematics, Biology
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/free_energy_principle|Free Energy Principle]], [[cognitive/active_inference|Active Inference]], [[knowledge_base/cognitive/homeostatic_regulation|Homeostatic Regulation]]

### Functional Connectivity
**Definition**: Statistical dependence between different brain regions based on correlated activity.

**Domains**: Neuroscience, Cognitive Science, Mathematics
**Related Terms**: [[knowledge_base/free_energy_principle/implementations/neural_networks|Neural Networks]], Correlation Analysis, [[systems/network_analysis|Network Analysis]]

## G

### Game Theory
**Definition**: Mathematical study of strategic decision-making in situations where outcomes depend on choices of multiple agents.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Economics, Social Science
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/decision_making|Decision Making]], Multi-Agent Systems, Nash Equilibrium

### Generative Model
**Definition**: Probabilistic model that can generate data samples similar to observed data.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Statistics, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/probabilistic_models|Probabilistic Models]], [[cognitive/generative_model|Generative Model]], [[mathematics/bayesian_networks|Bayesian Networks]]

### Graphical Model
**Definition**: Probabilistic model represented as a graph, where nodes represent variables and edges represent dependencies.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/bayesian_networks|Bayesian Networks]], [[mathematics/markov_random_fields|Markov Random Fields]], Causal Modeling

## H

### Hierarchical Inference
**Definition**: Multi-level process of drawing conclusions, where higher levels provide context for lower-level processing.

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Mathematics, Computer Science
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/hierarchical_inference|Hierarchical Inference]], Multi-Scale Analysis, Hierarchical Systems

### Homeostasis
**Definition**: Process by which biological systems maintain internal stability despite external changes.

**Domains**: Biology, Physiology, Systems Theory
**Related Terms**: [[biology/homeostasis|Homeostasis]], Feedback Control, Physiological Regulation

### Human-Computer Interaction
**Definition**: Study of how people interact with computers and design of interfaces that facilitate effective interaction.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Psychology, Design
**Related Terms**: User Interface Design, Human-Machine Systems, Usability

## I

### Information Theory
**Definition**: Mathematical study of quantification, storage, and communication of information.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Computer Science, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/information_theory|Information Theory]], [[mathematics/entropy|Entropy]], [[cognitive/information_processing|Information Processing]]

### Intelligence Amplification
**Definition**: Use of technology to enhance human cognitive abilities beyond natural limitations.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Cognitive Science, Philosophy
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/artificial_intelligence|Artificial Intelligence]], Human Enhancement, Human-Machine Integration

### Interoception
**Definition**: Perception of internal bodily states, including physiological conditions and emotional feelings.

**Domains**: Neuroscience, Psychology, Biology
**Related Terms**: Bodily Awareness, Physiological Sensing, Emotion

## K

### Knowledge Representation
**Definition**: Methods for storing and organizing knowledge in a form that can be used by computer systems.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Cognitive Science, Philosophy
**Related Terms**: Knowledge Representation, Semantic Networks, Theory of Meaning

## L

### Learning Theory
**Definition**: Mathematical study of how systems can learn from data to improve performance.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Computer Science, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[knowledge_base/cognitive/statistical_learning|Statistical Learning]], [[docs/repo_docs/machine_learning|Machine Learning]], [[systems/adaptive_systems|Adaptive Systems]]

### Long-Term Potentiation
**Definition**: Long-lasting strengthening of synapses between neurons, fundamental to learning and memory.

**Domains**: Neuroscience, Biology, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[knowledge_base/cognitive/synaptic_plasticity|Synaptic Plasticity]], Memory Formation, [[cognitive/learning_mechanisms|Learning Mechanisms]]

## M

### Machine Learning
**Definition**: Study of algorithms that can learn from and make predictions on data without being explicitly programmed.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Statistics, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[docs/repo_docs/machine_learning|Machine Learning]], Statistical Inference, [[knowledge_base/cognitive/pattern_recognition|Pattern Recognition]]

### Markov Chain
**Definition**: Mathematical system that undergoes transitions from one state to another according to probabilistic rules.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/stochastic_processes|Stochastic Processes]], State Transitions, Sequential Processing

### Mental Model
**Definition**: Internal representation of external reality that people use to understand and interact with the world.

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Psychology, Philosophy
**Related Terms**: Mental Models, Knowledge Representation, Theory of Mind

### Message Passing
**Definition**: Algorithm for computing marginal probabilities by exchanging messages between variables in a graphical model.

**Domains**: Mathematics, Computer Science, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/belief_propagation|Belief Propagation]], Inference, Distributed Computation

### Multi-Agent System
**Definition**: System composed of multiple interacting intelligent agents that can coordinate to achieve goals.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Systems Theory, Social Science
**Related Terms**: Multi-Agent Systems, Collective Intelligence, Distributed Intelligence

## N

### Natural Language Processing

**Domains**: Computer Science, Linguistics, Cognitive Science
**Related Terms**: [[cognitive/language_processing|Language Processing]], Computational Linguistics, Semantic Understanding

### Neural Network
**Definition**: Computational model inspired by biological neural networks, consisting of interconnected nodes that process information.

**Domains**: Computer Science, Neuroscience, Mathematics
**Related Terms**: [[knowledge_base/free_energy_principle/implementations/neural_networks|Neural Networks]], [[biology/neural_systems|Neural Systems]], Optimization

### Neuromodulation
**Definition**: Alteration of nerve activity through chemical or electrical means, affecting neural excitability and synaptic transmission.

**Domains**: Neuroscience, Biology, Medicine
**Related Terms**: Neural Modulation, Attention Modulation, Neural Control

**Domains**: Mathematics, Computer Science, Engineering
**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/optimization_theory|Optimization Theory]], [[knowledge_base/mathematics/control_theory|Control Theory]], Decision Optimization

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Neuroscience, Psychology

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Computer Science, Systems Theory

**Domains**: Computer Science, Cognitive Science, Psychology

**Related Terms**: [[systems/systems_theory|Systems Theory]], Holism, Systems Modeling

**Domains**: Cognitive Science, Psychology, Philosophy

**Related Terms**: [[mathematics/game_theory|Game Theory]], Competitive Behavior, Conflict Resolution
