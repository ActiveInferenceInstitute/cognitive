# Evolutionary Biology

## Overview

Evolutionary biology studies the processes that produced the diversity of life — natural selection, genetic drift, mutation, and gene flow. Under the Free Energy Principle, evolution can be understood as free energy minimization over phylogenetic timescales, where species that persist are those whose phenotypic repertoire implicitly minimizes surprise (i.e., maintains adaptive fitness).

## Key Concepts

### Natural Selection as FEP
- Selection as a process that minimizes long-term average surprise
- Fitness landscapes as free energy landscapes
- Niche construction as active inference at evolutionary timescales

### Evolutionary Dynamics
- Population-level dynamics and their mathematical formalization
- Replicator dynamics and evolutionary game theory
- Detailed in [[evolutionary_dynamics|evolutionary dynamics]]

### Evo-Devo and Development
- Evolution of developmental processes under the FEP
- Morphogenesis as active inference — see [[morphogenesis]]
- Developmental constraints as structural priors
