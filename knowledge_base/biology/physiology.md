# Physiology

## Overview

Physiology is the study of how biological systems function at molecular, cellular, organ, and organismic levels. In the context of the Free Energy Principle, physiological processes can be understood as implementing homeostatic and allostatic control — the body's regulatory mechanisms continuously minimize free energy by keeping internal states aligned with the organism's generative model of viability.

## Key Concepts

### Interoceptive Inference
- The brain's inference about internal bodily states
- Interoceptive predictions driving autonomic regulation
- Connection to [[knowledge_base/cognitive/active_inference|active inference]] and embodied cognition

### Autonomic Regulation
- Sympathetic and parasympathetic control as competing hypotheses
- Vagal tone as a measure of regulatory capacity
- Allostatic load and chronic free energy elevation

### Metabolic Physiology
- Energy regulation as a fundamental homeostatic process
- Glucose homeostasis and predictive metabolic control
- Related to [[homeostasis]] and [[allostatic_regulation]]
