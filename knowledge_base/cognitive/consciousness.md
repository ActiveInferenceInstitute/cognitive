# Consciousness


## Overview

Consciousness encompasses the subjective experience of awareness, both of the external world and internal mental states. It includes phenomenal experience (what it feels like), access consciousness (availability for cognitive processing), and self-awareness (metacognitive understanding of one's own mental states).

## Core Components

### Levels of Consciousness

- consciousness levels - Awareness states

  - [[wakefulness]] - Level of arousal and responsiveness

    - [[alertness]] - Highest arousal, ready to respond

    - drowsiness - Reduced awareness

  - altered states - Modified consciousness

    - sleep states - Consciousness changes across sleep stages

    - meditative states - States induced by meditation practice

### Contents of Consciousness

- conscious contents - Experience types

  - sensory consciousness - Perceptual awareness

    - visual awareness - Sight experience

    - auditory awareness - Sound experience

  - cognitive consciousness - Thought awareness

    - thought awareness - Awareness of one's own thoughts

    - [[emotional_awareness]] - Awareness of one's own emotions

### Self-Awareness

- [[self_consciousness]] - Self-knowledge

  - bodily awareness - Awareness of one's own body

    - [[body_schema]] - Body representation

    - [[interoception]] - Internal sensing

  - [[metacognition]] - Awareness of one's own mental processes

    - [[self_reflection]] - Inner examination

    - agency awareness - Action ownership

### Neuromodulation

- consciousness modulation - Regulation of conscious state by brain systems

  - arousal systems - Brain systems maintaining wakefulness

    - [[reticular_activation]] - Brainstem network driving cortical arousal

    - thalamic regulation - Thalamic control of information flow to cortex

  - neurotransmitter systems - Chemical signaling that modulates awareness

    - [[cholinergic]] - Attention/arousal

    - monoaminergic - Dopamine, serotonin, and norepinephrine systems

## Theoretical Frameworks

### Information Integration Theory

- [[integrated_information]] - Theory linking consciousness to integrated information

  - [[phi_measure]] - Integration metric

    - [[causal_structure]] - Cause-effect

    - information structure - Structure of cause-effect relations in a system

  - [[integrated_information|exclusion principle]] - Only one maximally integrated boundary is conscious

    - spatiotemporal grain - Spatial and temporal resolution of conscious elements

    - [[causal_exclusion]] - Only the maximal cause-effect structure counts

### Global Workspace Theory

- [[global_workspace]] - Theory of conscious access through broadcast

  - workspace dynamics - Activity patterns of the global workspace

    - competition - Content selection

    - [[broadcast]] - Information sharing

  - [[access_consciousness]] - Information available for reasoning and report

    - reportability - Ability to report an experience

    - cognitive access - Availability of content to cognitive systems

### Predictive Processing

- conscious prediction - Predictive processing account of consciousness

  - prediction hierarchy - Layered predictions from abstract to concrete

    - [[top_down_predictions]] - Expectations

    - bottom up errors - Prediction errors passed up the hierarchy

  - conscious inference - Experience as inference about hidden causes

    - [[perceptual_inference]] - Inferring causes of sensory input

    - [[active_inference]] - Acting to fulfill predictions and reduce uncertainty

## Experimental Paradigms

### Behavioral Methods

- consciousness tasks - Tasks for studying and measuring awareness

  - report paradigms - Explicit measures

    - subjective reports - Participants' own accounts of experience

    - [[confidence_ratings]] - Participants' self-rated certainty

  - implicit measures - Indirect tests

    - [[priming_effects]] - Influence of stimuli not consciously perceived

    - [[blindsight]] - Visual responses without reported awareness

### Neural Measures

- consciousness imaging - Imaging brain activity during awareness tasks

  - contrastive analysis - Comparing conscious and unconscious conditions

    - seen unseen - Contrast between seen and unseen stimuli

    - conscious unconscious - Contrast between conscious and unconscious processing

  - [[connectivity_analysis]] - Measuring interactions between brain regions

    - [[effective_connectivity]] - Causal influence of one region on another

    - [[functional_connectivity]] - Statistical correlation between regional activity

### Manipulation Methods

- consciousness manipulation - Experimentally altering conscious state

  - stimulation methods - Direct stimulation of brain tissue

    - [[tms]] - Transcranial magnetic stimulation

    - tdes - Transcranial electrical stimulation

  - pharmacological - Drug-based manipulation

    - [[anesthetics]] - Drugs that suppress consciousness

    - [[psychedelics]] - Drugs that alter perception and cognition

## Clinical Applications

### Disorders of Consciousness

- consciousness disorders - Conditions with impaired consciousness

  - [[coma]] - Unresponsiveness

    - [[vegetative_state]] - Wakefulness without awareness

    - [[minimal_consciousness]] - Fluctuating but definite awareness

  - locked in - Awareness intact

    - complete - Total paralysis

    - incomplete - Partial movement

### Assessment Tools

- consciousness assessment - Evaluation

  - behavioral scales - Bedside scales rating behavioral responses

    - [[coma_scale]] - Scale rating eye, verbal, and motor responses

    - awareness scale - Scale rating level of awareness

  - brain measures - Neural tests

    - eeg markers - EEG signatures of conscious state

    - imaging markers - Imaging signatures of conscious state

### Treatment Approaches

- consciousness treatment - Intervention

  - pharmacological - Drug therapy

    - [[stimulants]] - Drugs that raise arousal

    - modulators - Drugs that adjust neurotransmitter activity

  - stimulation - Brain stimulation therapy

    - deep brain - Implanted electrodes stimulating deep structures

    - transcranial - Non-invasive stimulation through the skull
