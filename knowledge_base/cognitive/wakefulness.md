# Wakefulness

## Overview
Wakefulness is the state of being awake and responsive to the environment, as opposed to sleep or coma. Together with awareness (the contents of experience), it is one of the two dimensions in standard clinical descriptions of consciousness: level and content. The two can come apart, since patients in unresponsive wakefulness have preserved sleep-wake cycles and eye opening with little or no evidence of awareness.

## Mechanisms
- **Ascending arousal system.** Moruzzi and Magoun showed that stimulating the brainstem reticular formation produces cortical activation. Later work identified several neurochemical populations: noradrenergic (locus coeruleus), serotonergic, cholinergic, dopaminergic, histaminergic, and orexin/hypocretin neurons, which together promote cortical and behavioural arousal.
- **Sleep-wake regulation.** The two-process model (Borbély) holds that sleep timing depends on a homeostatic pressure that builds during waking and a circadian process driven by the suprachiasmatic nucleus.
- **Electrophysiology.** Waking EEG is dominated by low-amplitude, faster activity; slow oscillations typify non-REM sleep, and REM sleep combines activated EEG with muscle atonia.
- **Graded states.** Wakefulness is not binary: drowsiness, alertness, and hyperarousal differ in performance. The relation between arousal and performance is classically described as an inverted U ([[vigilance]], [[sustained_attention]]).
- **Functions of waking.** Waking supports interaction with the environment and the encoding of experience that sleep then consolidates ([[memory_consolidation]], [[synaptic_consolidation]]).

## Role in Active Inference
Hobson and Friston proposed that waking consciousness is a process of inferring the causes of sensory input, while sleep, and REM sleep in particular, offers a state without sensory constraints in which the generative model can be refined by reducing complexity. Under this view, the waking state is distinguished by high precision on sensory prediction errors, and dreaming by the dominance of priors ([[precision_weighting]]). The proposal is a theoretical account with some supporting simulations; it is not an established explanation of sleep function, and competing theories exist ([[sleep_memory_ai]]).
