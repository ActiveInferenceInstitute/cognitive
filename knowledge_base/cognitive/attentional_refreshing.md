# Attentional Refreshing

## Overview
Attentional refreshing is a proposed mechanism for maintaining information in working memory. Attention is directed briefly to a representation that was just active, such as a recently seen item, and this "thinking of" it is held to increase its accessibility without overt speech or articulation. It is usually contrasted with articulatory rehearsal, which recycles verbal material through the phonological loop (see [[verbal_rehearsal]]).

## Evidence and models
Johnson and colleagues (2002) asked participants to think of a word that had just been presented and found that this reflective refreshing improved later memory, with an age-related deficit in older adults. Neuroimaging of the same operation implicated left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex. The time-based resource-sharing model (Barrouillet, Bernardin & Camos, 2004) gives refreshing a formal role. Memory traces are assumed to decay while attention is occupied by a concurrent task. Attention can restore them in the gaps, so performance depends on the proportion of time that processing captures attention, called cognitive load. Predictions of this model have been supported in span tasks that vary processing time.

Whether refreshing is a distinct process remains debated. Some accounts treat it as a form of covert retrieval or as attention to a representation in the focus of attention, and others dispute that decay exists at all and attribute forgetting to interference. Reviews of working memory describe refreshing as one candidate among several maintenance mechanisms (Baddeley, 2012).

## Role in Active Inference
The link is an analogy. In predictive processing, attention is described as raising the precision of prediction errors or of beliefs about a source of information (see [[precision_weighting]]). Maintenance could then be read as repeatedly boosting the precision of a belief that would otherwise drift toward its prior. No established Active Inference model reproduces the specific predictions of the time-based resource-sharing account, and the mapping should be read as a hypothesis.
