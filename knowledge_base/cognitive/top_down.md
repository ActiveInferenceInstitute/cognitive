# Top-Down Processing

## Overview
Top-down processing is processing in which higher-level information (expectations, goals, knowledge, context) influences how lower-level sensory or perceptual information is analysed. It contrasts with bottom-up processing, which is driven by the stimulus. In cognitive science the term describes both information flow, via feedback connections, and the cause of an effect, such as attention directed by a goal. The pages linking here also use "top-down" for hierarchical organisation (high versus low levels); this page covers the cognitive sense.

## Evidence
- **Context effects.** Letters are recognised more accurately inside words than alone (word superiority), and ambiguous shapes take the interpretation the context favours.
- **Attention.** Goal-directed attention biases visual cortex toward task-relevant features and locations, often distinguished from stimulus-driven capture ([[selective_attention]], [[endogenous_attention]]).
- **Perceptual expectation.** Priors shift detection and appearance, for example faster recognition of expected stimuli and the resolution of degraded images once the content is known.
- **Anatomy.** Feedback connections in cortex are numerous throughout sensory hierarchies, giving a substrate for such influences.
- **Penetrability debate.** Whether cognition can alter perception itself, as opposed to attention, memory, or response bias, is disputed, and critics argue many reported demonstrations have confounds.

## Role in Active Inference
Top-down signals are the central object of predictive coding: each level sends predictions about the activity below it, and the bottom-up channel carries prediction errors, as in the model of Rao and Ballard ([[predictive_coding]], [[prediction_error]]). Perception is then the settling of hypotheses at all levels, and attention corresponds to the precision assigned to errors, so it modulates top-down influence ([[precision_weighting]]). This is a framework with a modelling basis; the anatomical assignment of predictions and errors to specific laminae and pathways is still under test.
