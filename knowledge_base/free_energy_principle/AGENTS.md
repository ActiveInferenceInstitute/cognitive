# Free Energy Principle Knowledge Base

The free energy principle (FEP) domain organizes the repository's treatment
of the FEP across seven subdomains: applications, biology, cognitive science,
implementations, mathematics, philosophy, and systems. Each subfolder carries
its own short `AGENTS.md` index and document list.

## Subdomains

- `applications/` — FEP applied across disciplines.
- `biology/` — biological formulations of the FEP.
- `cognitive/` — cognitive and neuroscientific treatments.
- `implementations/` — algorithmic and modeling implementations.
- `mathematics/` — formal and mathematical foundations.
- `philosophy/` — conceptual and philosophical analysis.
- `systems/` — systems-level FEP treatments.

## Notes

- The domain `README.md` is the navigation entry point.
- Pages are conceptual; pseudocode within them is illustrative and is not
  package API. Executable implementations live in the `cognitive` and
  `Things` packages.
- Authoring rules for knowledge-base pages are in
  `knowledge_base/AGENTS.md`.
