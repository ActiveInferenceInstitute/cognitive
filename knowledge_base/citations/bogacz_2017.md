# Bogacz (2017)

**A tutorial on the free-energy framework for modelling perception and learning**

## Reference

Bogacz, R. (2017). A tutorial on the free-energy framework for modelling perception and learning. *Journal of Mathematical Psychology*, 76, 198-211.

## Key Contributions

- Accessible, step-by-step tutorial introduction to the free energy framework
- Clear derivation of predictive coding from variational free energy minimization
- Practical examples connecting mathematical formalism to neural implementations
- Bridge between computational neuroscience and the Free Energy Principle

## Relevance to Active Inference

This tutorial is one of the most widely recommended entry points for understanding the mathematical foundations of the Free Energy Principle. It clarifies how perceptual inference can be cast as free energy minimization, laying the groundwork for the more general active inference framework that extends these principles to action and planning.
