# Amari (2000)

**Methods of Information Geometry**

## Reference

Amari, S., & Nagaoka, H. (2000). *Methods of Information Geometry*. Translations of Mathematical Monographs, Vol. 191. American Mathematical Society.

## Key Contributions

- Foundational treatment of information geometry as a mathematical discipline
- Development of the Fisher information metric on statistical manifolds
- Natural gradient methods for optimization on manifold structures
- Dual connections and their role in statistical inference

## Relevance to Active Inference

Information geometry provides the geometric framework underlying variational inference in active inference. The Fisher information metric defines the natural geometry of belief spaces, and natural gradient descent offers principled methods for belief updating that respect this geometry. These tools are essential for understanding the mathematical structure of free energy minimization.
