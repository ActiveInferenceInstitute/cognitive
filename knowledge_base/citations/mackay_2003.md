# MacKay (2003)

**Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms**

## Reference

MacKay, D. J. C. (2003). *Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms*. Cambridge University Press.

## Key Contributions

- Unified treatment of information theory, Bayesian inference, and machine learning
- Clear exposition of variational methods and approximate inference
- Connections between coding theory, statistical physics, and learning
- Accessible introduction to Monte Carlo methods and graphical models

## Relevance to Active Inference

MacKay's textbook provides foundational coverage of the information-theoretic and Bayesian tools that underpin the Free Energy Principle. Concepts such as KL divergence, variational inference, model evidence, and the relationship between compression and prediction are central to understanding how active inference agents minimize surprise through belief updating.
