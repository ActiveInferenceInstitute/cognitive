# Sutton & Barto (2018)

**Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd Edition)**

## Reference

Sutton, R. S., & Barto, A. G. (2018). *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* (2nd ed.). MIT Press.

## Key Contributions

- Comprehensive textbook treatment of reinforcement learning theory and algorithms
- Temporal difference learning, policy gradient methods, and model-based RL
- Multi-armed bandit problems and the exploration-exploitation tradeoff
- Function approximation and deep reinforcement learning foundations

## Relevance to Active Inference

Reinforcement learning serves as both a comparison framework and a complement to active inference. Active inference subsumes aspects of RL by casting reward maximization as a special case of free energy minimization, where preferred outcomes (priors over observations) replace explicit reward functions. Understanding RL provides essential context for appreciating how active inference unifies perception, learning, and decision-making under a single objective.
