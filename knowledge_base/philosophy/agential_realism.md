# Agential Realism

## Overview
Agential realism is the position developed by the physicist and feminist theorist Karen Barad, most fully in *Meeting the Universe Halfway: Quantum Physics and the Entanglement of Matter and Meaning* (2007). It draws on the philosophy-physics of Niels Bohr and on feminist science studies to argue against the assumption that the world consists of independently existing objects with inherent boundaries that are then observed or interacted with. The position is a central reference in the new materialisms.

## Main concepts
- **Phenomena.** For Barad the smallest unit of analysis is a phenomenon, which she describes as a specific intra-action of an "object" and the "measuring agencies" (Barad, 2007, p. 128, as quoted in the New Materialism almanac). The object and the agencies of observation are not given beforehand but emerge from the intra-action.
- **Intra-action.** Barad coined the term in contrast to interaction, which presupposes separate entities that exist prior to their relation. Agency, on this view, is a matter of intra-acting and is enacted within phenomena, not possessed by a pre-existing actor.
- **Agential cut.** Separations such as subject and object, or measured system and instrument, are enacted within phenomena by an agential cut, a momentary stabilisation described as a cutting together and apart. Nothing is taken to be inherently separate, but determinate boundaries are produced.
- **Apparatuses.** Measurement apparatuses are material-discursive practices that help to produce determinate phenomena and meanings, while excluding others. This is Barad's reading of Bohr's insistence that the experimental arrangement is part of what defines a measured quantity.
- **Onto-epistemology.** Because what is known and the means of knowing are co-constituted, Barad treats ontology, epistemology and ethics as inseparable.

## Diffraction as method
Diffractive methodology, a term associated with Barad and with Donna Haraway, takes its image from the pattern that forms when waves meet an obstacle. In contrast to reflection, which suggests mirroring and sameness, diffraction directs attention to differences and to the effects that research practices themselves produce. Readings of different texts or disciplines, such as physics and feminist theory, are read through one another instead of one being applied to the other.

## Debate
Agential realism is a philosophical position that draws on quantum physics, not a result of physics. Its influence has been largest in cultural studies and social theory, and how well it fits the physics and the interpretive literature on quantum theory is contested (see [[measurement_problem]] and [[philosophy_of_physics]]).

## Relation to Active Inference
The connection is interpretive and slight. The Markov blanket formalism also separates a system from its environment by a statistical boundary that is a modelling choice, which can be compared to the agential cut as a boundary enacted within a larger whole. Active Inference, however, is standardly formulated with classical probability and makes no claims drawn from quantum theory in Barad's sense, and Barad does not rely on the free-energy formalism. Treat the comparison as a prompt for discussion.
