# Ontology Knowledge Base Agents

The ontology domain collects conceptual frameworks for knowledge
organization: how the repository's domains and concepts are structured and
related, including cognitive, biological, mathematical, philosophical, and
systems ontologies.

## Documents

- [[cognitive_ontology]] — structures for cognitive concepts.
- [[biological_ontology]] — biological structure and relations.
- [[mathematical_ontology]] — mathematical objects and foundations.
- [[philosophical_ontology]] — metaphysical frameworks.
- [[systems_ontology]] — systems-level organization.
- [[computer_science_ontology]] — computational structures.
- `hyperspatial/` — higher-dimensional ontology notes.

## Notes

- The pages are conceptual; see `README.md` for navigation.
- Ontology pages define terms used elsewhere in the knowledge base; link to
  them rather than duplicating definitions.
- Authoring rules for knowledge-base pages are in
  `knowledge_base/AGENTS.md`.
