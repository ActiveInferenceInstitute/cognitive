# Active Inference Institute

The Active Inference Institute is a non-profit organization that supports
learning, teaching, and research on Active Inference, the framework rooted in
the Free Energy Principle for modelling how systems minimise uncertainty
through perception and action. This page is a short external summary. The
dated year-by-year history, funding and accreditation details, programme
counts, and 2026 to 2029 roadmap that an earlier version carried were not
backed by any source in this repository and have been removed; the Institute's
own website is the authority for them.

## Activities

- Livestreams and workshops across machine learning, computational modelling,
  psychology, philosophy, biology, and related fields.
- Textbook study groups, for example on Parr, Pezzulo and Friston, *Active
  Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior*.
- Internships and fellowships for mentored research.
- Volunteer contributions to study groups, livestream production, and
  publications.

## Collaborations

- Nature Based AI Joint Initiative with the Boston Global Forum, which aims to
  bring natural systems and neuroscience into AI development; see the
  [announcement](https://dukakis.org/shaping-futures/nature-based-ai-joint-initiative-science-technology-policy-boston-global-forum-active-inference-institute/).

## Getting involved
