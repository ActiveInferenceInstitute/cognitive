# Knowledge base and graph license

Repository-owned content in `knowledge_base/` and the RDF graph exports generated
from it are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license (SPDX identifier `CC-BY-4.0`):

- [License and attribution requirements](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- [Governing legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)

This grant permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial reuse, with
appropriate attribution, a link to the license, and an indication of changes.
The Active Inference Institute approved this licensing scope on 2026-10-13 for
Open Knowledge Network integration. Earlier releases retain their original notices.

Suggested attribution: "Cognitive Active Inference knowledge base, Active
Inference Institute contributors, https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/cognitive,
CC BY 4.0." Retain page-level source links and identify the source revision and
any modifications when redistributing an export.

This license covers the repository's own explanatory material and graph metadata.
References to papers, external identifiers, and linked resources do not relicense
the external works themselves. The maintainer reported no known third-party
content exceptions on 2026-10-13. Contributors must have authority to contribute
their content under CC BY 4.0 and retain attribution to any incorporated material.

Software remains MIT under [LICENSE](LICENSE). Documentation outside
`knowledge_base/` remains CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Generated graph samples and statistics
under `docs/okn/` are graph export artifacts covered by CC BY 4.0.
