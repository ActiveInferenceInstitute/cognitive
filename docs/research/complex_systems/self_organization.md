# Self-organization

Theory only. The free energy principle's account of self-organization to a
non-equilibrium steady state is written up in
[[knowledge_base/mathematics/non_equilibrium_steady_state]],
[[knowledge_base/cognitive/free_energy_principle]], and
[[knowledge_base/cognitive/emergence_self_organization]]. The package computes
free energies for small models; it does not simulate self-organizing systems.
