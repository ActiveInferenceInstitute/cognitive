# Research guidelines

The rules for research pages are in [[docs/research/AGENTS|the research
instructions]]: no invented results, every repository claim points at a real file
or command, and unbuilt work is labelled as a proposal. General guidance is in
[[docs/guides/research_guides|research guides]] and
[[docs/guides/best_practices|best practices]].
